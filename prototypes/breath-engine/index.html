The check-up

          
How well do you breathe when you're not trying?

        

        
A simple measure used by breathing coaches everywhere:
          after a normal exhale, how many seconds pass before your body first
          asks for air? Not a contest — you stop at the first clear urge,
          not your limit. Higher scores mean calmer everyday breathing, and the
          practice moves this number.

        

          
Sit down for this. It's a gentle test, not a challenge.
            Skip it if you're pregnant or have heart or blood-pressure conditions.
