# Breath Coach — Recording Script

**For the voice artist. Version 1.0 — 66 lines, one session (~half a day).**

## How to read this document

- Record **3 takes per line**: (1) as directed, (2) softer and more intimate, (3) your own instinct.
- Say the line ID once before the first take only, then leave a second of silence before each take.
- Every line ends with a **falling tone** — let sentences land, never lift.
- `…` means a real pause — half a second or more. Silence is part of the performance.
- **Max** is the hard time limit for the spoken line at your natural pace. If a take runs over, slow down your *pauses*, not your words — or retake.
- A few lines differ slightly from what appears on screen in the app; the version here is the one to record.
- General pace is slow — around **100–110 words per minute**, roughly two-thirds of normal conversation. It should feel almost too slow in the booth. It won't be.

Work one experience at a time, in this order. Re-read the direction block aloud before starting each one.

## 1 · THE UNWIND — for the end of a long day

**Direction.** You're a close friend in the armchair opposite, lamps low, the day finished. Warm, settled, faintly amused at how wound up humans get. Low in your range, unhurried, a soft half-smile you can hear. Nothing to prove, nowhere to be.
**Avoid:** sounding sleepy. You're relaxed, not tired.

**UNW-LEAD** · Max 8s
> "Nothing to fix for the next four minutes. … We'll begin on your next breath."

**UNW-S1** · Max 8s
> "Let the chair take your full weight."
*Land "full weight" like you're setting something down.*

**UNW-S2** · Max 8s
> "You don't have to breathe well. … Just breathe along."

**UNW-D1** · Max 8s
> "Make the exhale a slow pour, … not a push."
*"Pour" is the word that matters — let it stretch.*

**UNW-D2** · Max 8s
> "Somewhere on this breath out, let your jaw unclench."
*Let your own jaw actually loosen on this one. It's audible.*

**UNW-D3** · Max 8s
> "Each exhale drops your shoulders a little further."

**UNW-D4** · Max 8s
> "The day is over. … Tell your body — it's the last to hear."
*(Shortened from the on-screen line — record this version.)*

**UNW-C1** · Max 8s
> "Two more. … Make this one the softest yet."

**UNW-C2** · Max 8s
> "Last one. … Empty all the way out."
*Your quietest line of the set.*

**Phase cues** — spoken at the top of a breath, calm and brief:

| ID | Line | Max |
|---|---|---|
| UNW-CUE-IN | "Breathe in" | 1.5s |
| UNW-CUE-OUT | "Let it go" | 1.5s |

**Optional closing** — UNW-CLOSE · Max 8s
> "Whatever's left of the day … it can wait until tomorrow."

## 2 · FIRST LIGHT — for before the day starts

**Direction.** Early morning, first coffee, window light. Quiet optimism with a slight audible smile. Lighter and clearer than The Unwind, but just as unhurried. You're not waking anyone up — you're keeping the morning calm on their behalf.
**Avoid:** breakfast-radio energy. The smile is small.

**FL-LEAD** · Max 8s
> "Before the phone, before the list. … We'll begin on your next breath."

**FL-S1** · Max 9s
> "Even in, even out. … Like drawing a slow circle."

**FL-S2** · Max 9s
> "No pushing. The rhythm does the work."

**FL-D1** · Max 9s
> "This pace is yours. … The day will try to change it."
*(Shortened from the on-screen line — record this version.)*

**FL-D2** · Max 9s
> "Notice the turn at the top of the breath. … Smooth it."

**FL-D3** · Max 9s
> "Awake is not the same as wired. … This is awake."
*Slight emphasis on the second "awake" — that's the whole pitch.*

**FL-D4** · Max 9s
> "One thing at a time today, … starting with this breath."

**FL-C1** · Max 9s
> "A few more. … Memorise this tempo."

**FL-C2** · Max 9s
> "Last one. … Carry it out the door with you."

**Phase cues:**

| ID | Line | Max |
|---|---|---|
| FL-CUE-IN | "Breathe in" | 1.5s |
| FL-CUE-OUT | "Breathe out" | 1.5s |

**Optional closing** — FL-CLOSE · Max 9s
> "The day gets to join your rhythm today — not the other way round."

## 3 · STEADY GROUND — for the moment before something big

**Direction.** A trusted cornerman before the walk-out. Firm, even, completely undramatic — steadiness demonstrated, not described. Slightly more forward and articulate than the others; every word placed, none pushed. You believe in this person and it's not in question.
**Avoid:** drill-sergeant edge, and pep-talk lift. Flat certainty.

**SG-LEAD** · Max 8s
> "Sit tall. Feet on the floor. … We'll begin on your next breath."

**SG-S1** · Max 14s
> "Four equal sides. … Nothing rushed, nothing dragged."

**SG-S2** · Max 14s
> "Feel your feet. … That's the ground you're standing on."

**SG-D1** · Max 14s
> "The holds are where the steadiness is built."

**SG-D2** · Max 14s
> "Same pace in, same pace out. … You set the tempo now."
*Small weight on "you."*

**SG-D3** · Max 14s
> "Whatever's coming, … it gets this version of you."
*The line of the set. Total conviction, zero drama.*

**SG-D4** · Max 14s
> "Square by square, the noise loses interest."

**SG-C1** · Max 14s
> "Two more squares. … Keep them clean."

**SG-C2** · Max 14s
> "Last one. … Walk out of it steady."

**Phase cues** — these get four, one per side of the square:

| ID | Line | Max |
|---|---|---|
| SG-CUE-IN | "Breathe in" | 1.5s |
| SG-CUE-HOLD | "Hold" | 1.2s |
| SG-CUE-OUT | "Let it go" | 1.5s |
| SG-CUE-HOLD2 | "Stay empty" | 1.2s |

**Optional closing** — SG-CLOSE · Max 14s
> "The room hasn't changed. … You have."

## 4 · THE RELEASE VALVE — for when it's all too much, right now

**Direction.** A paramedic's calm. The listener is mid-overwhelm; you are present, certain, and completely unhurried — which is exactly what tells their body the emergency is over. Closer to the mic, a touch more direct than The Unwind. Short lines, fully landed.
**Avoid:** urgency, and sympathy-voice. You're not worried, so they don't have to be.

**RV-LEAD** · Max 8s
> "This takes ninety seconds. You have ninety seconds. … We'll begin on your next breath."
*Matter-of-fact on the second sentence — it's permission, not a countdown.*

**RV-S1** · Max 7s
> "Two sips of air in, one long pour out."

**RV-D1** · Max 7s
> "That second sip pops your lungs open. … That's the trick."

**RV-D2** · Max 7s
> "The exhale is the valve. … Open it slowly."

**RV-D3** · Max 7s
> "You're not solving anything right now. Just releasing the grip."

**RV-C1** · Max 7s
> "Two more. … Longest exhales yet."

**RV-C2** · Max 7s
> "Last one. … Let everything leave with it."

**Phase cues** — the sip cue is the tightest timing in the whole session:

| ID | Line | Max |
|---|---|---|
| RV-CUE-IN | "Breathe in" | 1.2s |
| RV-CUE-SIP | "A little more" | **1.0s — hard cap.** Quick and light, almost tossed away. If it won't fit, record "…more" alone as RV-CUE-SIP-ALT. |
| RV-CUE-OUT | "Let it all go" | 1.5s |

**Optional closing** — RV-CLOSE · Max 7s
> "Still a lot on your plate — but you're holding it with looser hands."

## 5 · THE DRIFT — for the edge of sleep *(record last)*

**Direction.** You're reading a bedtime story and you're half-asleep yourself. The most intimate read of the session: 8–10 cm from the mic, breathy, barely above a murmur — but still **intelligible**; this is whisper-*adjacent*, not whispering. Slowest of everything: ~85–95 wpm, long pauses, consonants soft. It's fine — good, even — if your own sleepiness shows by now.
**Avoid:** actual whisper (it hisses), and "spa voice" breathiness on every word. Let gravity do it.

**DR-LEAD** · Max 8s
> "Lie down. Let the bed do the holding. … We'll begin on your next breath."

**DR-S1** · Max 17s
> "Sink. … The bed can carry all of it."

**DR-S2** · Max 17s
> "Eyes closed, … if they want to close."

**DR-D1** · Max 17s
> "On the hold, do nothing. … Floating counts."

**DR-D2** · Max 17s
> "Let the exhale sound like a sigh. … No one's listening."

**DR-D3** · Max 17s
> "Each wave takes you a little further from shore."

**DR-D4** · Max 17s
> "You don't need to fall asleep. … Just stop holding yourself awake."
*The most important line in the experience. Take your time with the pause.*

**DR-C1** · Max 17s
> "Nearly there. … Heavier now."

**DR-C2** · Max 17s
> "Last one. … Let it lower you all the way down."

**Phase cues** — softest cues of the session:

| ID | Line | Max |
|---|---|---|
| DR-CUE-IN | "Breathe in" | 1.5s |
| DR-CUE-HOLD | "Rest here" | 1.2s |
| DR-CUE-OUT | "Sigh it out" | 1.5s |

**Optional closing** — DR-CLOSE · Max 17s
> "If sleep comes, let it. … You're done here."

## 6 · UNIVERSAL LINES

Neutral-warm register — closest to The Unwind's tone, usable after any experience.

**UNI-RELEASE** *(plays as the session ends, orb going still)* · Max 6s
> "And now … let your breath come back to its own rhythm."

**UNI-RETURN** *(plays on the reflection screen)* · Max 6s
> "Take one normal breath. … Nothing guided. Just yours."

## 7 · BREATH SOUNDS *(very end of the session, after all voice work)*

You'll breathe on-mic at exact durations, 8–10 cm distance. Use a **silent stopwatch in your eyeline** — no audible click. Each breath must sound *relaxed at that length*; if one sounds like effort, rest and retake. Three clean takes each. No congestion, no whistle — if your nose isn't cooperating today, we re-record these another day.

| ID | What | Duration | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-IN-2 | inhale through the nose | 2.0s | easy, unforced |
| BR-IN-4 | inhale through the nose | 4.0s | slow, faintly audible |
| BR-IN-55 | inhale through the nose | 5.5s | long and even, no strain at the top |
| BR-SIP-1 | a second short "sip" of air on top of a full breath | 1.0s | light, quick |
| BR-OUT-4 | exhale through the nose | 4.0s | controlled, quiet |
| BR-OUT-55 | exhale through the nose | 5.5s | even all the way down |
| BR-OUT-6 | exhale through soft open lips — a gentle "haa" | 6.0s | barely voiced, releasing |
| BR-OUT-8 | long sighing exhale, mouth | 8.0s | a hint of voiced sigh at the start, fading to pure air |
